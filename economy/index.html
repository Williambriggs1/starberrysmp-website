💰 Currency & Economy

StarberrySMP uses a simple player-driven economy built around trading, QuickShop chest shops, PlayerWarps, and a dependable server bank.

Player Shops & Bank

Players can build markets and destinations with QuickShop and PlayerWarps. The bank provides a steady way to convert selected useful resources into money while player-to-player trading remains the heart of the economy.

The current baseline is 1 Diamond = $100, with selected custom crops and resources potentially added as additional bank-selling options.
