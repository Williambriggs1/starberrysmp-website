🌱 Custom Crops

Grow custom crops that expand farming without replacing vanilla Minecraft. New crops unlock alongside Farming progression and become ingredients for Starberry recipes.

A Growing Crop Collection

The Starberry crop collection includes berries, vegetables, herbs, grains, fruit, and other custom ingredients. Each crop has its own lore and can be used in one or more custom foods.
