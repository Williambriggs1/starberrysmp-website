⭐ Skills

StarberrySMP rewards the things you already do in survival. Farming, Mining, Foraging, and Fishing level naturally through normal Minecraft gameplay.

Vanilla-Friendly Progression

There are no classes or jobs to select. As you farm, mine, gather wood, and fish, your corresponding skills grow and gradually unlock useful milestones that complement normal survival.
