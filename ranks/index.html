🍓 Ranks & Supporter

StarberrySMP starts every player at Seed and offers four supporter tiers focused on quality-of-life features and personalization rather than pay-to-win progression.

Seed → Sprout → Bloom → Berry → Starfruit

Rank perks include increasing SetHome and QuickShop limits, with convenience features such as nickname access and Bloom's teleport-only /back command. Higher tiers add additional customization and supporter conveniences.
