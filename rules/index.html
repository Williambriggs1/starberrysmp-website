📜 Server Rules

StarberrySMP is a welcoming PG-13 Minecraft community. Our rules cover respectful conduct, safety and privacy, claims and property, fair gameplay, trading, accounts, and staff support.

Minecraft Community Rules

Players should respect each other, protect personal information, avoid griefing and stealing, play fairly without cheats or exploit abuse, trade honestly, follow alternate-account limits, and use Starling for support when needed.

Each player may use up to one Java account and one Bedrock account, but only one account may be online at a time. Alternate accounts cannot be used to bypass limits, progression, rewards, or punishments.
